## Design Brief

Write a concise brief:

> Design a [page type] for [company/product], targeting [audience], to communicate [core message] and achieve [business goal]. The page should feel [brand attributes], organize content around [main structure], and be validated through [prototype / visual direction / stakeholder review].

## Success Criteria

- Users understand what the company/product does.
- Users understand why it is credible or differentiated.
- The page structure can be reviewed by stakeholders.
- The visual direction can be turned into a high-fidelity design.
- The output can be validated as a prototype before frontend development.
