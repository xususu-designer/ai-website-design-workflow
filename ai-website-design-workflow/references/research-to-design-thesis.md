## Research Evidence Rules

- Use current source checks when competitors, references, product facts, claims, laws, prices, or industry context may have changed.
- Prefer primary sources: official websites, product docs, annual reports, investor pages, case studies, app screenshots, or user-provided materials.
- Record source URL or local file path, access date when relevant, observed pattern, reusable principle, and what must not be copied.
- Do not copy visual identity, proprietary diagrams, wording, metrics, or layouts from references.
- Mark unsourced market claims as assumptions.
