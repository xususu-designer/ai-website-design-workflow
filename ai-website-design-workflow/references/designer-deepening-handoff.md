## Implementation Readiness Checklist

- Final copy is public-facing and approved enough for design use.
- Product names and terminology are normalized.
- Design uses real content length, not placeholder labels.
- Desktop and mobile frames are present or responsive rules are documented.
- Assets, icons, images, and diagrams have either source files or replacement rules.
- Any designer-only exploration notes are outside the actual page frames.
